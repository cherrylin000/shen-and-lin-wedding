关于婚礼，我们想了很久。

人生有许多重要的时刻，人们总盼着热闹些、圆满些。可想来想去，我们还是觉得，婚姻是两个人的事，最要紧的，是彼此认定了对方。

于是，我们做了一个小小的决定。

去世界的尽头，办一场只有我们两个人的婚礼。

没有满堂宾客，也没有繁复的仪式。天地作证，山海为席，我们郑重地把往后的日子，交到彼此手中。

只是，两个人的婚礼，并不意味着只有两个人的牵挂。

一路走来，承蒙父母养育，也有幸与你们相识相伴。那些爱与祝福，早已是我们生命的一部分。

所以，我们想把这一天好好地留存下来，也想邀请你们，隔着山海，与我们一同见证。

若你恰好有闲，不妨慢慢往下翻，看看我们的故事，看看一路走来的模样。

若你时间匆忙，也无妨。

翻至最后一页，便可预约婚礼直播。

不必远行，不必盛装。

To Our Dearest Family and Friends,

We hope this letter finds you well.

We've spent a long time thinking about our wedding.

There are moments in life that seem to call for grand celebrations, surrounded by everyone we love. But the more we thought about it, the more we felt that marriage, at its heart, is simply about two people choosing each other.

So we made a little decision.

To travel to the other side of the world, and have a wedding with just the two of us.

No grand gathering, no elaborate traditions. Just the two of us, in a quiet church, making a promise to share whatever life brings.

But a wedding for two doesn't mean there are only two people in our hearts.

We owe so much to our parents, who raised us with love, and to the family and friends who have been part of our lives. Every kindness, every shared memory, every word of encouragement has helped bring us to where we are today.

And so, while we've chosen a quiet ceremony, we'd still love to share this day with you.

If you have a little time, scroll down and take a look at our story, and the moments that brought us here.

And if life is keeping you busy, that's perfectly fine too.

Simply turn to the last page to reserve a spot for our wedding livestream.

There's no need to travel, or dress up for the occasion.

When the day comes, we hope you'll join us from wherever you are, and share a little of our happiness.

Though miles may lie between us, we're glad to have you with us in spirit.

Thank you for being part of our story.

And thank you for being here as we begin its next chapter.

Hang & Hanyun

Life Together

🤵: Man, time flies! Three years, just like that. Looking back, we've both changed so much. Especially me, right? I'm practically a whole new man! Come on, babe, how handsome is your man now?

👰: You've definitely made a lot of changes for me, and I'll give you credit for that. But weren't you the one starting all those arguments? I don't care, most of them were your fault!

You should be grateful that I'm gorgeous, kind-hearted, AND willing to reason with you. Seriously, you hit the jackpot marrying me!

🤵: Alright, alright. My bad, my bad. Honestly, we barely have anything to argue about these days. It's getting a little boring!

👰: Wanna fight, then?

Oh wait, no. We're supposed to be telling our love story. Ahem.

The truth is, we don't really have some epic, movie-worthy romance.

If I had to describe it, I'd say it's about being there for each other when we're vulnerable, and choosing to stay when things get tough.

There have been spontaneous getaways, unexpected surprises, and plenty of nights spent gaming or binge-watching shows together. Turns out we're pretty much the same kind of weird.

And yes, we've had our fair share of arguments. But someone always makes the first move to make up. And I have to give Hang credit for that. It's usually him.

We've both changed a lot for each other over the years, and our family and friends have seen it happen.

I wouldn't call us soulmates, and I don't believe we were lovers in some past life. We've simply learned to pull out a few of our own thorns, soften our rough edges, and find a way to fit together without losing the freedom to be ourselves.
